About this site

We publish only figures that we could confirm on the pages of Japan Customs, the Ministry of Finance and the National Tax Agency. Where online explainers contradict each other, we went to the official documents and wrote down which one is right. When the rules change, we update the articles and record it in the update log.

This site is written for people who order from overseas to Japan, including residents and visitors. The official sources are in Japanese, so we link to them and say so each time.

This is general information. The actual amount of tax is decided by Customs.
